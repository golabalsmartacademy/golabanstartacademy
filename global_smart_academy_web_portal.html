Flagship Feature

Smart Transport & Live Parent Notification System

Every school vehicle is equipped with hardware GPS tracking and student QR Code scanners. Parents receive automated instant notifications for boarding & alighting.

1. Live GPS Bus Tracking

Parents can log into the Parent Portal to monitor real-time vehicle movement on Google Maps, estimated time of arrival (ETA), and route status.

2. Student QR Code Verification

Authorized conductors scan student QR ID cards upon entry and exit. Ensures no child is left behind or dropped at the wrong stop.

3. Instant Entry & Exit Alerts

Automated triggers push immediate alerts to parents' mobile devices (WhatsApp/SMS) the exact second their child boards or exits the bus.
